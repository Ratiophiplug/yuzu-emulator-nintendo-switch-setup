## 🌟 About

**Yuzu Emulator — Nintendo Switch Setup** is a complete guide and toolkit that walks you through installing and configuring Yuzu, the popular open-source Nintendo Switch emulator, on your PC.

From firmware installation to controller mapping and graphics tweaks — everything you need to go from zero to playing, all in one place.
